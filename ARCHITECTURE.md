# Citadel Architecture Reconstruction

## Primary massing

The working reconstruction treats the Citadel as a large trans-dimensional city/station organized around a dominant central transparent/glass dome, several smaller domed districts and a much larger lower metallic structural body.

## Modeling principles

1. Build the global silhouette first.
2. Establish a normalized coordinate system and master scale.
3. Separate structural shell from city/decorative layers.
4. Use radial/circumferential organization where views support it.
5. Model repeated building families parametrically where possible.
6. Keep uncertain geometry modular so it can be revised without rebuilding the whole model.

## Geometry layers

- L0 — global station shell
- L1 — domes and major superstructure
- L2 — primary streets and transit
- L3 — district blocks
- L4 — landmark buildings
- L5 — interior plazas/vegetation
- L6 — props, signage and small infrastructure
- L7 — destruction/reconstruction variants

## Coordinate system

Use a right-handed coordinate system with the primary Citadel vertical axis as Z. Establish the central dome center at the origin. Maintain a separate canonical scale factor so later dimensions can be revised without changing topology.

## Validation

Every major modeled surface should be tagged with its evidence source and confidence level. Render the model from matching camera angles and compare silhouette, dome placement, district relationships and skyline density against reference imagery.
