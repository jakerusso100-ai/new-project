# Citadel Canon Timeline

This document separates observed Citadel states from reconstruction inference.

## Major Citadel appearances

- **Close Rick-Counters of the Rick Kind** — foundational Citadel introduction, Council of Ricks and Citadel exterior/interior references.
- **The Rickshank Rickdemption** — Citadel aftermath and destruction context.
- **The Ricklantis Mixup** — extensive city-life reference: streets, districts, commerce, transit, policing and social infrastructure.
- **Rickmurai Jack** — major late-Citadel material and Citadel history/context.
- **Solaricks** — post-destruction/aftermath context.
- **JuRicksic Mort** — later Citadel-related continuity context.
- **The Rick, The Mort & The Ugly** — later Citadel continuity reference.

## State tracking

Each future reference entry should record:

- episode/comic
- scene/page
- camera/view direction if known
- visible structures
- confirmed geometry
- inferred geometry
- contradictions with other views
- confidence level

## Confidence labels

**A — Direct:** geometry clearly visible and repeated or unambiguous.

**B — Strong inference:** geometry follows from multiple compatible views.

**C — Plausible:** required to connect known spaces but not directly shown.

**D — Speculative:** design completion with insufficient canonical evidence.
