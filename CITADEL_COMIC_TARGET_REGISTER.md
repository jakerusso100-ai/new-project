# Citadel Comic Target Register

Purpose: identify comic material that must be checked at panel/page level before it can be used as architectural evidence. Comic continuity is kept separate from animated-series evidence.

## Priority targets

### 1. Rick and Morty Presents: The Council of Ricks #1 (Oni Press, 2020)

Known Citadel locations include:
- Downtown Rickville
- Rickingham Palace
- Citadel subsections containing a gaggle of Jerrys

Research targets:
- exterior establishing views
- street/building views of Downtown Rickville
- Rickingham Palace exterior and interior
- subsection entrances/corridors
- Council-related spaces
- security/military spaces
- any aerial or wide views
- any signage that identifies districts

The available issue synopsis explicitly identifies Downtown Rickville and Rickingham Palace as Citadel locations and refers to Citadel subsections. This establishes the names in comic continuity but does **not** establish their placement in the animated Citadel.

### 2. A Tale of Two Jerries / Doofus Jerry storyline

Target the Citadel scenes surrounding Doofus Jerry's takeover.

Research targets:
- Citadel militia deployment
- Council/judicial response
- citywide technology used by Doofus Jerry
- Citadel structures shown during the takeover
- any wide exterior or streetscape panels

Use only exact panel/page evidence for geometry. Do not promote a comic-only arrangement into animated continuity without corroboration.

### 3. It's a Kind of Morty Magic!

Comic-index sources identify this as another Citadel appearance. It requires panel-level verification before architectural details are accepted.

Research targets:
- whether the Citadel is visibly shown
- exterior geometry
- named locations
- streets/buildings
- government/security architecture
- any cross-sections or unusual structural views

### 4. Pocket Like You Stole It

The Citadel is associated with Morty bloodsport in the comic material.

Research targets:
- arena/bloodsport architecture if visibly depicted
- access corridors or spectator areas
- relationship, if any, to other Citadel facilities

Treat this as comic supplemental evidence unless independently corroborated by the animated series.

## Evidence rules

- `COMIC-CANON`: directly visible/identified in the cited comic.
- `COMIC-SUPPLEMENTAL`: useful architectural/context evidence but not animated continuity.
- `SHOW-CORROBORATED`: comic feature independently visible in the show.
- `UNVERIFIED-COMIC`: identified by an index/synopsis but not yet checked panel-by-panel.
