# Civic Systems

The Citadel should be reconstructed as a functioning city-state, not only as a shell. This register captures systems demonstrated by the story.

## Government
- Council of Ricks is the earlier governing structure.
- Council Hall is a distinct civic/government space.
- Later Citadel governance includes presidential elections and a President Morty administration.
- Government therefore requires public-facing civic space, administrative space, security, records and controlled-access areas where supported by evidence.

## Police / security
- Citadel militia is a major security organization.
- Ricks and Mortys can serve in police/security roles.
- Police facilities include operational and training functions.
- Security must account for ordinary policing and high-level portal/technology threats.

## Education
- Morty Academy trains and houses Mortys awaiting assignment.
- Graduation/assignment is an institutional process.
- The school is physically part of Citadel society rather than an off-site service.

## Population / Morty administration
- The Citadel supports systems for Morty allocation/assignment.
- Morty Agency/database evidence establishes administrative infrastructure for this process.
- Do not infer an exact database-room footprint beyond visible evidence.

## Courts / law
- The Citadel has judicial functions including a courthouse/judges.
- Judicial areas should be treated as a separate civic function from police facilities.

## Economy
- Retail, cafes, entertainment, insurance, childcare, industrial production and criminal economies all appear.
- Simple Rick's provides industrial employment and manufacturing.
- The city therefore needs commercial streets plus industrial/service districts.

## Agriculture / food
- Farm/Mega Fruit scenes demonstrate internal agricultural production.
- Natural water and terrain can coexist with developed infrastructure.

## Waste
- The Wishing Portal is revealed as a waste-disposal system.
- Waste infrastructure is therefore a real city utility and not merely a background joke.

## Social stratification
- Rick and Morty populations occupy visibly different social/economic roles.
- Mortytown is especially important evidence for lower-status residential/commercial urbanism.
- Avoid assigning every building to a class without direct evidence.
