# Citadel Comic Cross-Reference

Purpose: capture comic evidence that can inform a Citadel reconstruction without treating comic continuity as identical to the animated continuity.

## Known Citadel comic appearances / references

### Rick and Morty Presents: The Council of Ricks (2020)

- Citadel is a major setting.
- Rick Prime is sent to investigate counterfeit Ricks.
- Five counterfeit Ricks enter the Citadel.
- The story identifies **Downtown Rickville** and **Rickingham Palace** as Citadel locations.
- The story references **subsections of the Citadel** containing a gaggle of Jerrys.
- Council-era characters and political structures provide additional evidence for the Citadel as a large organized city rather than a single government building.

### A Tale of Two Jerries / Doofus Jerry storyline

- The Citadel is used as a major setting in the comic storyline involving Doofus Jerry.
- The animated-series reference material describes the Citadel militia responding to Doofus Jerry and the Council attempting to try him; Doofus Jerry subsequently uses the Genetic Restructurer to take control of the Citadel.
- Treat this as **comic-continuity evidence**, not as proof of the animated Citadel's exact geometry.
- Architectural value: useful for identifying additional civic/security/political functions that the comic continuity associates with the Citadel.

### Rick and Morty in: It's a Kind of Morty Magic!

- Comic-industry location indexes list the Citadel among its appearances/references.
- This issue remains a **high-priority visual verification target**: individual panels should be inspected before extracting geometry or named locations.
- Until panel-level evidence is logged, do not treat unverified index metadata as proof of a specific Citadel layout.

### Pocket Like You Stole It

- The Citadel is associated with Pocket Morty bloodsport in the comic material.
- This is useful as supplemental evidence for organized entertainment/combat activity, but it should not be used to infer a specific arena footprint without a panel-level source record.

## Comic evidence policy

1. Record the exact issue/title before using an image as evidence.
2. Separate animated-series canon from comic continuity.
3. A comic location can establish that a place/concept exists in that comic continuity without establishing its placement in the animated Citadel.
4. Use comic visuals for architectural vocabulary, recurring Citadel motifs, and supplemental clues unless the same feature is independently supported by the show.
5. Do not copy complete copyrighted comic pages into the repository. Record page/issue identifiers, descriptions, and lawful source links instead.
6. Do not infer geometry from an index entry alone; panel-level visual evidence is required for geometry claims.
