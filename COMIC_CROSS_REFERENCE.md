# Citadel Comic Cross-Reference

Purpose: capture comic evidence that can inform a Citadel reconstruction without treating comic continuity as identical to the animated continuity.

## Known Citadel comic appearances / references

### Rick and Morty Presents: The Council of Ricks (2020)

- Citadel is a major setting.
- Rick Prime is sent to investigate counterfeit Ricks.
- Five counterfeit Ricks enter the Citadel.
- The story identifies **Downtown Rickville** and **Rickingham Palace** as Citadel locations.
- The story references **subsections of the Citadel** containing a gaggle of Jerrys.
- Council-era characters and political structures provide additional evidence for the Citadel as a large organized city rather than a single government building.

### Rick and Morty comic appearances

Comic Vine catalogs the Citadel of Ricks as appearing in three comic issues/appearances, including *A Tale of Two Jerries, Part Two: That Thing You Doofus* and *Rick and Morty in: It's a Kind of Morty Magic!* and the *Presents: The Council of Ricks* issue.

## Comic evidence policy

1. Record the exact issue/title before using an image as evidence.
2. Separate animated-series canon from comic continuity.
3. A comic location can establish that a place/concept exists in that comic continuity without establishing its placement in the animated Citadel.
4. Use comic visuals for architectural vocabulary, recurring Citadel motifs, and supplemental clues unless the same feature is independently supported by the show.
5. Do not copy complete copyrighted comic pages into the repository. Record page/issue identifiers, descriptions, and lawful source links instead.

## High-value comic targets for further study
