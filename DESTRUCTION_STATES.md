# Citadel Destruction / Reconstruction State Register

Purpose: separate physical states seen across continuity. Similar-looking structures are not assumed to persist unchanged between states.

| State ID | Era/source | Directly established | Research consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| DS-01 | Early Citadel | Functioning city, Council/government, urban infrastructure | Baseline evidence set |
| DS-02 | Post-Rickshank | Severe damage followed by active rebuilding | Damaged shell and repair evidence must remain separate from baseline |
| DS-03 | Ricklantis rebuilt | Streets, commerce, schools, police, industry, transit and agriculture functioning during reconstruction era | Strongest urban-life evidence set |
| DS-04 | Late/President Morty | Rebuilt city plus Citadel-scale Dimensional Drive operation and specialized underground systems | Late-state infrastructure evidence |
| DS-05 | Rickmurai destruction | Global structural failure, portal-fluid flooding, separation of Mortyburg and final explosion | Destruction mechanics and detachable-section evidence |
| DS-06 | Solaricks ruins | Broken platforms, piping, launch-chamber remains, debris and scavenger-accessible ruins | Surviving-feature evidence only; do not reconstruct unseen intact geometry from ruins |
| DS-07 | New Citadel attempt | Boss Hog/Big Rick attempts reconstruction around the wreckage | Separate architecture/state; resemblance does not prove original geometry |
| DS-08 | New Citadel destruction | Attempted rebuild is destroyed | Adds another ruin/destruction state, not evidence for original layout |

## State comparison rules

- A structure visible in one state is not automatically present in another.
- Repair scaffolding and replacement construction should not be mistaken for original shell geometry.
- Ruins can establish what survived the destruction sequence, but cannot establish everything that was destroyed.
- New Citadel construction is not a canonical restoration blueprint for the original Citadel unless a source explicitly demonstrates equivalence.
- When two states disagree, preserve both records and link the conflict in `CONTRADICTION_LOG.md`.

## Highest-value state transitions

### Early → post-Rickshank
Useful for identifying damage versus original construction.

### Post-Rickshank → Ricklantis
Useful for identifying what was repaired/replaced and how the functioning city operates.

### Ricklantis → Rickmurai Jack
Useful for identifying late-state additions and the Dimensional Drive/underground systems.

### Rickmurai Jack → Solaricks
Useful for destruction-to-ruin comparison.

### Solaricks → New Citadel
Useful for distinguishing reuse of wreckage from genuine restoration.
