## Dialogue discipline

- A spoken name establishes a name/function, not an exact footprint.
- A character saying “under,” “above,” “inside,” “outside,” “bottom,” “train,” or similar spatial language can establish a relationship only when the scene context makes the reference unambiguous.
- Do not convert metaphor, jokes or casual dialogue into engineering facts.
- When dialogue conflicts with visible geometry, preserve the conflict in `CONTRADICTION_LOG.md` rather than silently choosing one.
