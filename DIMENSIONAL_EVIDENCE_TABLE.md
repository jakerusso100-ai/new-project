## Measurement policy

Only record an absolute number when the source itself supplies a trustworthy measurement or a defensible real-world scale anchor. Otherwise retain dimensionless ratios and qualitative relationships.

## Final status

There is no evidence-supported canonical absolute dimension for the complete Citadel. This is an explicit research result, not a missing field to be filled by invention.
