# Citadel District Inventory

The reconstruction should treat the Citadel as a city rather than a single building.

## Known/important districts and facilities

- Mortytown / Morty residential-commercial areas
- Council/government complex
- Police/security facilities
- Schools / educational facilities
- Residential blocks
- Commercial streets and shops
- Simple Rick industrial/factory area
- Transit corridors and taxi infrastructure
- Public plazas and civic spaces
- Construction/maintenance zones

## Known businesses and landmarks to map

- Cafe Sanchez
- Morty Day Care
- Morty Games
- Morty Mart
- Simple Rick's Wafer Cookie factory
- The Creepy Morty
- Morty Agency
- Morty Insurance
- Big Rick's Gumbo Hutch

## Mapping method

For each district record its approximate location, visible boundaries, street pattern, building-height range, landmarks, transportation access and evidence confidence. Do not assume every named facility is in the same continuity state or geographic position across all Citadel versions.
