# Citadel Episode Evidence — S03E07 “The Ricklantis Mixup”

## Scope
This register records physical, civic, transportation, industrial and district evidence from the densest Citadel-city episode. It does not infer an unseen master plan.

## Rebuilt city condition

The episode opens on a Citadel still undergoing construction after the damage associated with the Rickshank event. The city contains damaged buildings and roadways while ordinary traffic continues.

**Direct implications:** repair activity and normal urban occupation coexist; the Citadel is a functioning city during reconstruction.

## Surface circulation

The episode directly depicts:
- ordinary roads;
- sidewalks/urban pedestrian circulation;
- conventional cars;
- flying vehicles/hover traffic;
- dense pedestrian streets;
- a train used for commuting.

This provides evidence that Citadel circulation is multimodal rather than portal-only.

## Civic/commercial streetscape

Cafe Sanchez is shown as an operating commercial location. The surrounding streets support pedestrian movement and taxi access. A taxi passes Campaign Manager Morty and picks up a suited Rick, demonstrating curbside/roadside vehicle interaction.

## Police and academy infrastructure

The Citadel Police Academy includes a shooting range where trainee Ricks practice against synthetic Gromflomites. Police cruisers operate on city streets and dispatch directs units to named street intersections.

The transcript identifies “Fifth and BURP Avenue” and explicitly associates the location with Mortytown. This is useful evidence that at least some Citadel streets have named intersections and that districts are integrated with the road network.

## Morty Academy

Morty Academy is shown as a functioning educational facility with lockers, classrooms, teachers and students. Later the school is shown changing curriculum under the new regime.

This establishes a dedicated institutional school environment rather than education being purely informal/residential.

## Rail/transit

A train carries commuting Ricks through the city. The train provides a distinct mass-transit system separate from flying vehicles and road traffic.

**Unknown:** complete rail topology, number of lines, station count, elevation and relation to the later Mortyburg train route.

## News and communications

Citadel Morning News operates from a dedicated newsroom. Broadcast reporting covers named districts and infrastructure-related problems such as gravity outages in East Sanchez Heights and uranium-powered cellular technology.

This establishes a citywide communications/media institution and gives evidence for named urban districts.

## Simple Rick’s industrial complex

The Simple Rick’s Wafer Cookie Factory is a major industrial workplace containing:
- assembly-line production;
- factory workers;
- supervisory offices;
- machinery connected to biological/brain extraction;
- a “Flavor Core” chamber;
- security/SWAT access;
- exterior media coverage.

The factory therefore provides strong evidence for a dedicated industrial district/building type with production, administration and security subspaces.

## Mortytown

Mortytown is depicted as a distinct urban district with streets, businesses, residences and criminal activity. Morty Mart is shown as a commercial establishment. Police patrols and investigations occur throughout the district.

The episode also shows a residential/criminal interior with a crib and multiple Mortys, establishing that Mortytown contains occupied interior residential spaces, not merely an exterior street set.

## MegaFruit Farm / agricultural area

A farm area produces MegaFruit and includes agricultural land, a farmer Rick and a robot dog. The episode establishes agriculture as part of Citadel life-support/economic infrastructure.

**Unknown:** total agricultural footprint, whether farming is centralized, and its relationship to other food-production systems.

## Morty Agency / later political continuity note

The episode does not itself establish the Morty Agency. It should not be back-projected into this episode without separate evidence. The later Unmortricken material independently identifies Morty Agency as a Citadel location.

## Airlock / exterior waste of bodies

During the regime transition, dead Ricks and Mortys are placed in airlock pods and expelled outside the Citadel. The exterior shot shows numerous bodies/documents floating in space.

This is direct evidence for an airlock-based external disposal mechanism for bodies during this state of the Citadel. It should not automatically be equated with the separate waste-disposal system.

## Political/architectural transition

The Shadow Council Hall is shown as a dedicated political interior. Exterior imagery shows new banners covering old Rick emblems, while police and agricultural workers receive new uniforms/badges.

This provides evidence for political control expressed through modification of existing civic architecture rather than requiring a wholly new city shell.
