# Citadel Episode Evidence — S05E10 “Rickmurai Jack”

Purpose: source-grounded evidence register for Citadel physical/infrastructure observations. This is **not** a schematic and does not define invented coordinates or dimensions.

## Scene: Citadel in chaos

Source: “Rickmurai Jack” transcript.

Observed / stated evidence:

- The Citadel has a **central plaza** during the collapse sequence.
- The city has a **citywide portal supply** that can be hacked/controlled.
- The Citadel has a **Presidential Building**; Rick and Morty exit it during the collapse.
- The interior urban environment includes streets and structures that can be traversed during the emergency.
- The Citadel contains a **bottom/lower escape route** leading into underground infrastructure.
- The city experiences widespread fires, structural failures, explosions and portal-fluid flooding during the final failure.

Evidence status: SHOW-CANON / directly depicted or stated.

## Scene: Portal-fluid production / mines

The characters descend beneath the city and encounter portal fluid being manufactured. The sequence establishes:

- A substantial **underground portal-fluid production facility** exists beneath the Citadel.
- Rick describes reaching the source of untainted portal fluid.
- Portal fluid is collected/processed in large containers and a large central vat.
- Deformed Mortys are forced to work in the underground production area.
- Rick refers to this underground environment as a “cave base.”
- The underground facility is connected to the Citadel's larger portal infrastructure rather than being an isolated unrelated location.

Important limitation: the episode does **not** provide enough evidence to derive a complete underground floor plan, dimensions, production capacity, or exact relationship between every chamber.

## Scene: Dimensional Drive

The episode explicitly identifies a **Citadel Dimensional Drive** and shows the drive chamber during Evil Morty's operation.

Established evidence:

- The Dimensional Drive is a major engineered system inside the Citadel.
- Rick states that Evil Morty may have needed the Citadel's Dimensional Drive schematics.
- Activation produces a large-scale energy/portal phenomenon capable of affecting the Central Finite Curve.
- The drive chamber contains a control area and a large glass-domed/control enclosure around the principal control interface.
- The chamber is large enough to contain the drive machinery, control systems and evacuation infrastructure.
- Operation Phoenix vats feed biological material into the drive process.
- A spacecraft is loaded into an **exit chute** and launched from the bottom of the Citadel.

Important limitation: exact chamber dimensions, machinery dimensions, floor elevations and external structural connections are not established by this episode alone.

## Scene: Plaza / tramway / lower city

The final activation causes portal fluid to burst from the ground and sweep through the city. The exterior sequence identifies:

- A **Citadel plaza**.
- A **tramway** or tram/rail transit route.
- Ground-level streets/urban circulation.
- Underground access points, including a **manhole** used by Rick and Morty.
- Sewers/underground passages large enough for deformed Mortys to emerge.
- A train route connecting the affected city area to Mortyburg.

The sequence is especially valuable because it links above-ground circulation, underground access and a detachable district in one continuous event.

## Scene: Mortyburg detachment

The characters reach **Mortyburg** by train. Rick activates a control labeled “DISENGAGE.”

Directly established:

- Mortyburg is a physically separable section of the Citadel.
- The detachable section has a closing entrance/interface before separation.
- The section separates from the main Citadel body.
- Thrusters/boosters activate to propel the detached section.
- The detached section can survive independently in space after separation.
- The sequence establishes that the connection between Mortyburg and the main Citadel is an engineered structural/transit interface, not merely a metaphorical district boundary.

Do **not** infer the exact connection geometry, number of attachment points, structural diameter, or internal service connections without additional evidence.

## Scene: Final Citadel destruction

The Citadel is shown tilting during the dimensional-drive event and ultimately exploding. The final sequence establishes:

- The Citadel can undergo substantial global structural deformation.
- Portal fluid can escape from internal infrastructure into the city.
- The Dimensional Drive chamber is physically integrated enough that its failure affects the surrounding Citadel.
- The remaining Citadel body is destroyed in the event.
