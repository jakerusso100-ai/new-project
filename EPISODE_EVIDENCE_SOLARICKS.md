# Citadel Episode Evidence — S06E01 “Solaricks”

## Scope
Source-grounded evidence only. This file records what the episode establishes about the post-destruction Citadel. It does not reconstruct missing geometry.

## Ruined Citadel approach

The episode establishes that the Citadel has been destroyed and remains as a large spatially coherent ruin accessible from space. Beth and Summer travel to the ruined Citadel in spacesuits, demonstrating that the surviving structure is exposed to vacuum rather than functioning as an intact pressurized city.

**Confidence:** A — directly depicted/stated.

## Surviving launch/technical chamber

The characters reach Evil Morty’s former launch chamber. The chamber is heavily ruined but remains recognizable as a technical/launch environment.

Observed details:
- broken piping remains along the platforms;
- broken glass remains in the chamber;
- platforms lead toward the launch track;
- the launch-track/chamber relationship survives the destruction sufficiently for characters to navigate it;
- scavengers are able to enter the ruins and search for usable material.

**Geometry limitation:** this proves survival of portions of the launch infrastructure, but not the original chamber dimensions, floor plan, or connection to the outer shell.

## Spatial continuity of ruins

The episode repeatedly places characters on physically distinct ruined chunks of the Citadel. This supports treating post-destruction evidence as fragmented remnants of the former structure rather than as a complete intact plan.

The surviving wreckage should therefore be catalogued as **state-specific evidence** and not used automatically to infer that every surviving fragment retained its original position.

## Beacon / navigation evidence

Summer places a beacon on the Citadel ruins to allow Rick to locate them. This establishes that the ruins are sufficiently spatially distinct and externally accessible to serve as a navigational target.

## Scavenger accessibility

A group of scavengers is present inside the ruined launch area looking for loot. This establishes traversable surviving interior volumes after the destruction event.

## Reconstruction-use facts

1. Citadel destruction leaves substantial physical debris rather than complete disappearance.
2. The launch/technical infrastructure can survive partially as a recognizable ruin.
3. Broken pipes and glass are visible in surviving technical areas.
4. Platforms and a launch track remain spatially legible.
5. Surviving internal volumes can be entered from the exterior ruins.
6. The destroyed Citadel is exposed to space.
7. Ruined fragments should be treated as a separate physical state from the intact Citadel.

## Unknown / not established by this episode

- exact amount of the original shell remaining;
- exact orientation of surviving fragments;
- exact original-to-ruin correspondence for individual debris pieces;
- complete launch chamber footprint;
- exact track dimensions;
- exact pipe routing;
- complete pressure/air-retention architecture;
- complete underground survival;
- exact relationship of the ruined chamber to the Dimensional Drive;
- exact global debris field dimensions.
