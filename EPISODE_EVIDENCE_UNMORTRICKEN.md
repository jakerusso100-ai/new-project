# Citadel Episode Evidence — S07E05 “Unmortricken”

## Scope
This file records Citadel architectural/location evidence from the episode and its flashback material. It does not infer a complete layout from the flashback.

## Morty Agency

The episode's flashback establishes a dedicated **Morty Agency** within the Citadel. The agency contains a database concerning Mortys and is staffed by Ricks.

Directly useful architectural facts:
- the agency is a dedicated institutional facility;
- it contains a controlled database/record system;
- it has staff/security personnel;
- access is sufficiently controlled that deleting a Morty's record requires entering the facility and interacting with its systems;
- the facility is part of the Citadel's broader administrative infrastructure.

## Agency security

The facility has at least two identifiable Rick personnel associated with it: a Counter Rick and Guard Rick. Their presence establishes a distinction between administrative/database functions and physical security.

## Destruction continuity

The Morty Agency is subsequently destroyed along with the Citadel. Therefore its physical evidence belongs to the intact/late Citadel state rather than the post-destruction ruins.

## Reconstruction-use classification

**A — Direct:** existence of Morty Agency as a Citadel location.

**A — Direct:** dedicated database/record infrastructure.

**A — Direct:** staffed facility with security presence.

**B — Strong inference:** the agency has controlled-access architecture because the narrative requires physical entry and security personnel.

**UNKNOWN:** footprint, floor count, relationship to Presidential Building, street address, district, exterior massing and connection to other administrative facilities.

## Do not infer

The episode does not establish that the Morty Agency is adjacent to the Council Hall, Presidential Building, Mortytown, Morty Academy or any particular dome. Those relationships remain unresolved until corroborating visual evidence is logged.
