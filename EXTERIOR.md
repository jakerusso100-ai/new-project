# Citadel Exterior

## Observed design language

- Dominant central transparent/glass dome.
- Multiple smaller domed or semi-domed masses around the main body.
- Large lower metallic/industrial structural volume.
- Dense urban fabric visible within and around the major shells.
- Strong radial/circular organization in several views.

## Reconstruction checklist

- [ ] Master silhouette
- [ ] Central dome proportions
- [ ] Secondary dome placement
- [ ] Lower shell and structural plates
- [ ] External docking/attachment structures
- [ ] Visible openings and portals
- [ ] Exterior city massing
- [ ] Camera-matched validation renders

## Rule

Do not assign exact real-world dimensions until enough references establish a stable scale relationship. Use ratios first; convert to absolute units only in the model specification.
