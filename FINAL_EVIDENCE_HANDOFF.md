## Major physical evidence available

### Global structure

The Citadel is treated as a large city/station rather than a single building. The evidence base covers the dominant transparent/glass dome, additional dome evidence, lower metallic structural mass, dense urban construction and multiple vertical/service layers.

### Urban environment

The evidence base establishes streets, sidewalks, pedestrians, mixed-height buildings, commerce, schools, police facilities, industry, agriculture, civic buildings and neighborhood-scale areas. S03E07 is the strongest urban-density reference set.

### Transportation

Ground roads, conventional vehicles, flying/hover vehicles, taxis and commuter rail are all established. Exact network topology and relative vertical placement are not established unless a source explicitly shows them.

### Government/security

Council, Presidential, judicial, police, militia, security and Shadow Council functions are documented. Individual buildings are kept separate unless a source establishes their physical connection.

### Industrial/technical

Simple Rick production, Flavor Core security, underground portal-fluid production, Dimensional Drive machinery, Operation Phoenix vats and launch/exit infrastructure are documented from their respective source states.

### Detachable section

Mortyburg is treated separately from Mortytown. Its engineered disengagement, interface closure and booster/thruster escape are direct destruction-sequence evidence.

### Destruction/ruins

Rickmurai Jack establishes the destruction event; Solaricks establishes accessible exposed ruins and surviving launch/technical infrastructure. Ruins are not used as a complete intact floor plan.

### Comics

Comic evidence is supplemental and continuity-separated. `Rick and Morty Presents: The Council of Ricks` establishes Downtown Rickville, Rickingham Palace and Citadel subsections. Comic-only spatial relationships must not be transferred into the animated layout without corroboration.

## How to treat geometry

- **A / Direct:** may be treated as source-established.
- **B / Strong inference:** may be used provisionally when multiple compatible references support it.
- **C / Plausible:** useful hypothesis only.
- **D / Unknown/speculative:** do not treat as canon geometry.

For every unsupported dimension or relationship, use `UNKNOWN / NOT SHOWN`.

## Final handoff rule

The repository is now complete as a research database. The owner's schematic can proceed using the evidence registers while preserving unresolved geometry as provisional.

Do not convert a convenient assumption into canon merely because it makes the schematic easier to draw.
