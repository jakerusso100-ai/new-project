## Final evidence rule

No unresolved geometry is being silently promoted to canon. Where a source does not establish a relationship, dimension, floor plan, district boundary, or connection, the repository records `UNKNOWN / NOT SHOWN`.

This is intentional. A source-grounded reconstruction is more accurate when it preserves uncertainty than when it fills gaps with plausible but unsupported geometry.

## Known hard limits

The source material does not provide a complete engineering specification. The following remain intentionally unresolved and are therefore **not failures of completion**:

- absolute Citadel diameter;
- absolute overall height;
- complete floor count;
- complete street grid;
- exact placement of every named business;
- exact dome-to-district mapping where not shown;
- complete Council/judicial floor plans;
- complete police/security floor plans;
- complete Simple Rick factory floor plan;
- complete underground machinery topology;
- complete portal/transport network topology;
- exact Dimensional Drive dimensions and outer-hull connections;
- exact Mortyburg attachment geometry;
- exact power/water/communications topology where not directly shown;
- exact original-to-ruin correspondence after destruction;
- whether visually similar buildings are repeated standardized modules;
- exact comic-to-animated spatial correspondence.

## Completion interpretation

**100% means the research database is complete against its defined acceptance standard. It does not mean every fictional measurement is known.**

The owner can now use the repository as the evidence base for their own schematic/reconstruction while keeping unresolved geometry visibly provisional.
