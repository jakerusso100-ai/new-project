# Citadel Infrastructure Reconstruction

## Systems to reconstruct

### Transportation
- Primary streets and pedestrian corridors
- Hover/taxi traffic
- Service and maintenance routes
- Portal/teleport access

### Power and dimensional systems
- Central Dimensional Drive
- Portal-fluid supply system
- Distributed power/utilities
- Machinery beneath/inside the shell

### Waste
- Dedicated waste-disposal infrastructure
- Wishing Portal / disposal portal
- Airlock/death-disposal systems

### Civic infrastructure
- Police/security network
- Courts/government
- Schools
- Commercial services
- Residential services

### Industrial
- Simple Rick manufacturing
- Construction/repair zones
- Morty production/cloning infrastructure where canonically shown

## Modeling rule

Infrastructure is modeled as connected systems, not isolated props. Any route or machine that must logically connect two shown locations is tagged as an inference until direct evidence is found.
