## Rules

- A named location does not automatically establish its physical coordinates.
- A business name establishes function before it establishes architecture.
- Repeated visual appearance increases confidence in building form but does not automatically establish city-wide placement.
- Locations appearing only in dialogue remain location/function evidence until visual evidence is found.
