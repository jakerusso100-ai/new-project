# Citadel Research Framework

## Reconstruction objective

Build a source-grounded architectural reconstruction rather than an invented generic space station. Every major geometric decision should be traceable to one or more visual references.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Direct animated-series establishing shots and repeated views.
2. Official production/marketing material when it depicts canonical Citadel geometry.
3. Published comic panels/pages depicting the Citadel.
4. Repeated environmental details across scenes.
5. Secondary reference indexes, used primarily for discovery.
6. Inference only when evidence is incomplete; inferred geometry must be labeled.

## Known visual categories to collect

- Global exterior silhouette and scale.
- Central glass/transparent dome.
- Smaller domed districts.
- Lower metallic structural mass.
- Radial and circumferential streets.
- High-rise and low-rise building clusters.
- Council/government complex.
- Mortytown.
- Industrial/factory areas, including Simple Rick production.
- Police/security facilities.
- Schools and residential districts.
- Transit and portal infrastructure.
- Interior greenery, plazas and fountains.
- Destruction state and surviving structures.
- Rebuilt/late-series Citadel state.

## Important constraint

The show frequently uses cinematic angles rather than engineering drawings. Do not treat a single perspective shot as a complete floor plan. Cross-reference multiple views before locking dimensions.
