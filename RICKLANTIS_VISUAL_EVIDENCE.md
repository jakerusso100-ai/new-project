# Ricklantis Mixup — Citadel Visual Evidence Register

## Scope
Source-grounded observations from **S3E7, “The Ricklantis Mixup / Tales from the Citadel.”** This file records visible infrastructure and spatial clues only; it does not create a schematic or infer exact coordinates.

## High-confidence visual evidence

### 1. Citywide construction/rebuild state
- The Citadel is shown undergoing reconstruction after the damage from **The Rickshank Rickdemption**.
- Damaged buildings and damaged roadways remain visible while construction crews repair structures.
- A construction sequence shows Ricks rebuilding a skyscraper with heavy structural material.
- **Reconstruction implication:** the city has enough permanent construction capacity, workforce, streets and utilities to repair large urban structures in place.

### 2. Mixed ground and aerial transportation
- Standard road vehicles and flying vehicles are both visible during the city montage.
- A hover/flying taxi service exists.
- A conventional street-level roadway network exists alongside aerial transportation.
- A wealthy Rick is shown traveling in a private flying car.
- **Reconstruction implication:** transportation is multi-layered, but the exact elevation and topology of the aerial routes are not established by this episode alone.

### 3. Dense pedestrian urbanism
- Large crowds of Ricks with Mortys intermixed are shown walking on Citadel streets.
- The city therefore contains pedestrian-accessible streets/sidewalk environments rather than being only a collection of isolated facilities.

### 4. Cafe Sanchez / commercial frontage
- Campaign Manager Morty leaves Cafe Sanchez with purchased coffee.
- He attempts to hail a taxi from the street.
- The taxi passes him and picks up a Rick in business attire instead.
- **Evidence value:** establishes Cafe Sanchez as a functioning street-front commercial location within the urban circulation environment.
- **Unknown:** exact district, building height, block dimensions and adjacency to other landmarks.

### 5. Police academy / shooting range
- A police academy contains an indoor shooting range.
- Trainee Ricks practice with weapons against synthetic Gromflomite targets.
- Police personnel use recognizable CRPD-related equipment/clothing.
- **Evidence value:** Citadel law enforcement has dedicated training infrastructure rather than using only improvised street facilities.

### 6. Morty Academy
- Mortys use a dedicated school building with lockers and classrooms.
- The school has a main entrance that can be locked.
- Students use books/lockers and are taught by Teacher Rick.
- Later in the episode the entrance is locked and the school is shown in the transition to the post-election regime.
- **Evidence value:** dedicated educational architecture is part of the Citadel's civic/residential environment.

### 7. Commuter rail / train
- A Citadel train is shown carrying many Ricks commuting to work.
- The train operates as a mass-transit system separate from private flying cars and taxis.
- **Evidence value:** establishes dedicated rail/commuter infrastructure.
- **Unknown:** track placement, whether the train is underground/elevated/ground-level, station design and network extent.

### 8. Citadel Morning News
- A dedicated newsroom/broadcast operation exists.
- The broadcast covers local infrastructure/political stories, including a gravity outage in East Sanchez Heights.
- **Evidence value:** named urban subdivision **East Sanchez Heights** is established by dialogue/news graphics, while the exact physical placement remains unknown.

### 9. Simple Rick's Wafer Cookie Factory
- Large industrial production space contains an assembly line staffed by Ricks.
- Factory workers operate specialized processing machinery.
- The factory contains a restricted **Flavor Core** chamber.
- The Flavor Core can be placed under lockdown and protected by a force-field-like barrier.
- Simple Rick is physically connected to specialized machinery through headgear/tubing.
- The factory has an exterior entrance/approach and a large assembly-line interior.
- **Evidence value:** industrial manufacturing is physically integrated into the Citadel economy and occupies a substantial dedicated complex.

### 10. Flavor Core / industrial security
- The Flavor Core is treated as a distinct secured chamber within the factory.
- Police/SWAT personnel approach it from outside while a negotiator communicates with the occupant.
- A heavy cutting tool is used to breach the chamber area.
- **Unknown:** exact floor plan, chamber dimensions, relationship to the factory shell and utility systems.

### 11. Mortytown
- Mortytown has its own street environment and local commercial activity.
- Police drive through its streets.
- Mortys gather in alleys and spray-paint walls.
- Mortytown is treated as a recognizable neighborhood/section rather than a single building.
- Morty Mart operates there.
- **Unknown:** boundary, street grid, vertical extent and relationship to central Citadel districts.

### 12. Morty Mart
- Morty Mart is a street-facing retail location.
- Its interior includes a checkout/store environment and at least one non-standard/augmented Morty employee.
- The store is directly accessed during a police investigation.
- **Evidence value:** confirms ordinary retail storefront architecture within Mortytown.

### 13. The Creepy Morty
- The Creepy Morty is a named commercial location with an exterior entrance.
- Police hovercars arrive outside after a major incident.
- **Unknown:** interior arrangement and exact relationship to surrounding blocks.

### 14. Megafruit Farm / agricultural edge
- A dedicated agricultural area produces Mega Fruit.
- Farmer Rick uses a weapon and robot dog while guarding the crop.
- The farm is shown as an open environment distinct from dense urban streets.
- **Evidence value:** the Citadel includes agricultural land/use, consistent with the previously identified farm-at-edge evidence.
- **Unknown:** total farm area, enclosure geometry and exact edge relationship to the main city.

### 15. Wishing Portal building
- Four Mortys approach a rusty-looking building surrounded by a tall circular wall.
- The entrance is gated/locked and can be breached.
- The building provides access to the Wishing Portal area.
- **Evidence value:** waste/disposal infrastructure has a visually distinct secured building rather than simply being an exposed portal in an ordinary street.
- **Unknown:** exact disposal machinery and destination geometry from this scene alone.

### 16. Shadow Council Hall
- A dedicated hall contains the Shadow Council.
- Members have designated seating positions.
- Later political imagery shows new Morty/Rick banners replacing older Rick emblems on buildings.
- **Evidence value:** political power has dedicated interior civic space and exterior institutional signage/banners.

### 17. Airlock / corpse disposal
- Dead Ricks and Mortys are placed in airlock pods outside the Citadel and expelled into space.
- The final exterior shot shows numerous bodies floating beyond the Citadel.
- **Evidence value:** dedicated airlock infrastructure exists for disposing of bodies and/or material into space.
- **Unknown:** number and location of airlocks elsewhere in the station.

2. Ground transportation, aerial transportation and rail/commuter transportation coexist.
3. Large industrial complexes can contain multiple internal security zones and specialized machinery.
4. Named neighborhoods exist, but their boundaries cannot be reconstructed from this episode alone.
5. The Citadel's post-Rickshank reconstruction was substantial enough to involve skyscraper-scale construction and roadway repair.
6. Political changes can alter exterior institutional signage without proving that the underlying building geometry changed.
