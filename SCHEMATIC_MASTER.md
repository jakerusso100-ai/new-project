# Master Citadel Schematic

## Status

This is the master engineering-style reconstruction document. Dimensions remain normalized until enough independent visual references establish stable scale.

## Coordinate system

- Origin: center of the principal dome.
- Z: Citadel vertical axis.
- X/Y: horizontal axes through the principal dome.
- Positive-radius measurements are preferred for circular/radial geometry.

## Layers

1. Outer structural shell.
2. Principal transparent dome.
3. Secondary dome/superstructure groups.
4. Primary circumferential circulation.
5. Primary radial circulation.
6. District blocks.
7. Landmark complexes.
8. Vertical infrastructure.
9. Underground/industrial machinery.
10. Damage and New Citadel variants.

## Required drawings

- `TOP_MASTER`: complete plan view.
- `SIDE_MASTER`: longitudinal elevation.
- `FRONT_MASTER`: transverse elevation.
- `SECTION_A`: principal dome and civic core.
- `SECTION_B`: urban shell and lower machinery.
- `SECTION_C`: underground/industrial district.
- `TRANSIT_MASTER`: roads, taxis, portal/transport infrastructure.
- `DISTRICT_MAP`: named districts and landmarks.

## Evidence discipline

A geometry feature is locked only after either (a) it is directly visible and unambiguous, or (b) at least two independent references support the same relationship. Inferred connectors remain editable and carry confidence C/D.

## Scale strategy

Use ratios first. Choose one canonical model unit after establishing a stable reference object, then maintain a conversion table so the model can be rescaled without changing topology.

## Completion gate

The schematic phase is complete only when every major exterior mass, principal circulation route, named district, landmark, major vertical layer and known Citadel state has a mapped location or an explicit `UNKNOWN/NOT SHOWN` designation.
