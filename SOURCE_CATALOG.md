# Source Catalog

## Animated series

### Close Rick-Counters of the Rick Kind
- Primary purpose: first major Citadel introduction.
- Capture: exterior silhouette, Council environment, internal city scale, portals and circulation.

### The Rickshank Rickdemption
- Primary purpose: destruction event and damaged Citadel state.
- Capture: structural damage, external relationships and surviving masses.

### The Ricklantis Mixup
- Primary purpose: densest urban reference set.
- Capture: streets, sidewalks, buildings, businesses, taxis, police, school, construction and social districts.

### Rickmurai Jack
- Primary purpose: late Citadel and President Morty-era material.
- Capture: government/political spaces, underground systems, machinery and destruction context.

### Solaricks
- Primary purpose: aftermath.
- Capture: ruins, debris, surviving machinery and external spatial context.

### JuRicksic Mort
- Primary purpose: later continuity context.
- Capture: any visible Citadel remnants or continuity clues.

### Unmortricken
- Primary purpose: later Citadel-related flashback/context.
- Capture: Morty Agency and Citadel-era visual details.

### The Rick, The Mort & The Ugly
- Primary purpose: New Citadel reconstruction context.
- Capture: Boss Hog Rick's reconstruction, surviving ruins and replacement architecture.

## Comics

Use published comic material as a secondary visual reference set. Record issue, page/panel and what architectural fact is actually visible. Do not treat comic-only geometry as automatically identical to animated-series canon.

Useful discovery indexes:

- Rick and Morty Wiki comic pages: https://rickandmorty.fandom.com/wiki/Category%3AComic_pages

Prefer primary material. Secondary pages are indexes, not substitutes for the original visual evidence.
