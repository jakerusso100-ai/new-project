## Core facts currently supported

The Citadel is described as a trans-dimensional city-state/station inhabited by Ricks and Mortys. The visual description identifies a central glass dome, three smaller domed districts, lower metallic structural plates, and a large internal city. The interior combines metal structures with natural elements such as trees and fountains. A Dimensional Drive/teleportation system occupies a central position. citeturn0search0
