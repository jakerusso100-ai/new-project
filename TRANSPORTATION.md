# Transportation and Circulation

## Street network
- Citadel scenes show dense roadways integrated into the city fabric.
- Roads support ordinary vehicles, police traffic and pedestrian circulation.
- Record intersections, road curvature, lane count and elevation only where visible.

## Hover traffic
- Hovering/flying taxis and vehicles appear in Citadel city scenes.
- Treat them as a distinct circulation layer rather than assuming every road is ground-only.
- Record visible takeoff/landing behavior, clearance, traffic density and relationship to buildings.

## Conventional vehicles
- Ground vehicles also operate in the Citadel.
- This implies conventional road infrastructure coexists with advanced hover transport.

## Pedestrians
- Dense pedestrian traffic is an important scale reference.
- Use repeated person-to-door, person-to-vehicle and person-to-street proportions when estimating relative dimensions.

## Police transportation
- Police vehicles and rapid-response movement are part of the Citadel's security system.
- Police facilities should have circulation appropriate to the vehicles actually shown.

## Portal access
- Personal portal travel is central to Citadel connectivity.
- Citadel-scale portal/dimensional infrastructure is separate from ordinary street transportation.
- Record every visible portal chamber, gate, conduit, platform and access-control feature rather than assuming they share one network.

## Vertical/elevated circulation
- The Citadel's dense architecture allows elevated structures and non-ground circulation.
- Where a frame shows a bridge, elevated road, platform or upper-level connection, record its endpoints and relative height without extrapolating unseen connections.

## Reconstruction data to extract from each transportation shot

1. Camera direction/viewpoint
2. Ground plane
3. Road/sidewalk boundary
4. Vehicle type
5. Vehicle size relative to people
6. Traffic direction
7. Elevated vs ground movement
8. Building clearance
9. Signage
10. Visible access points
11. Police/security presence
12. Confidence level
