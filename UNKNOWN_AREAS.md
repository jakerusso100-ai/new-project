# Unknown / Not Shown Register

A faithful reconstruction must preserve uncertainty instead of filling every gap with invented canon.

## Current unknowns

- Exact absolute dimensions of the full Citadel.
- Exact number of floors throughout the entire city.
- Complete street topology outside shown camera areas.
- Exact location of every named business.
- Complete relationship between every visible dome and internal district.
- Full Council complex floor plan.
- Full police facility floor plan.
- Full Simple Rick factory floor plan.
- Complete underground machinery layout.
- Exact portal/transport network topology.
- Complete geometry of the Citadel before its earliest depicted state.
- Exact geometry of every rebuilt-state structure.
- Whether visually similar structures represent identical repeated modules.

## Handling rule

Unknown areas receive explicit placeholders in the master schematic. They may later be resolved by additional references, but they must never be silently converted from unknown to canon.

## Confidence escalation

D -> C requires a functional/geometric reason.
C -> B requires multiple compatible references.
B -> A requires direct, unambiguous evidence.
