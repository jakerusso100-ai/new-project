# Citadel Utility / Service Systems

Purpose: collect physical utility evidence without inventing unseen engineering layouts.

## Portal / dimensional systems

- Personal portal travel is repeatedly associated with Citadel operations.
- The Citadel has city-scale portal infrastructure in the late-state destruction sequence.
- Portal-fluid production exists underground.
- The Dimensional Drive is a distinct Citadel-scale system.
- Keep portal access, portal-fluid production and Dimensional Drive as separate evidence IDs unless a source explicitly connects components.

## Waste disposal

- A dedicated Wishing Portal/waste facility is shown in the Citadel urban environment.
- Body/material disposal also uses airlock-type infrastructure in space.
- The exact destination of waste and the complete waste-processing network remain unresolved.

## Sewers / underground access

- Rickmurai Jack shows manhole/sewer access and underground passages large enough for movement.
- Underground infrastructure connects the urban surface to industrial/portal-related spaces.
- The complete sewer map is not shown.

## Industrial process utilities

Simple Rick's factory and the portal-fluid facility demonstrate:
- large machinery;
- specialized processing equipment;
- restricted technical zones;
- worker circulation;
- security/containment;
- conduits/tubing and machinery interfaces.

These establish functions and visual vocabulary, not complete utility routing.

## Transportation interfaces

- Ground roads;
- pedestrian streets/sidewalks;
- hover/flying traffic;
- commuter rail;
- portal access;
- Mortyburg's detachable transit/structural interface.

Do not assume these systems share a single central network.

## Agriculture / life-support clues

- MegaFruit agricultural activity is directly associated with the Citadel.
- Trees, fountains and other natural/landscaped elements appear in city environments.
- These observations establish internal ecological/food-production functions but do not establish the full life-support architecture.

## Power / communications / water

**UNKNOWN / NOT SHOWN at complete-system level.**

Individual machines may visibly use power, tubing, displays or communications, but the Citadel-wide generation/distribution networks are not established sufficiently for a canonical engineering map.

## Evidence rule

For every utility feature, record:
1. exact source;
2. visible component;
3. demonstrated function;
4. physical connections actually visible;
5. inferred connections separately;
6. unknown/unshown extent.
