# Vertical Layers

This register separates observed vertical systems so later reconstruction does not collapse the Citadel into one flat city plane.

## Exterior shell / upper structure
- Central transparent/glass dome is the strongest recurring upper-shell feature.
- Additional smaller domed districts/components project from the main mass.
- The exterior includes large metallic lower structures/plates.

## Surface city
- Dense buildings, streets, plazas, trees, fountains and businesses form the visible urban environment.
- Buildings vary in height and density.

## Elevated circulation
- Hover traffic and elevated structures create movement above ordinary ground circulation.
- Exact connections must be source-derived.

## Subsurface/service space
- Utility and technical infrastructure is implied/visible beneath the urban environment.
- Waste disposal requires large industrial/portal machinery.
- Do not assume all underground space belongs to the Dimensional Drive.

## Dimensional Drive / central machinery
- The Citadel has a large-scale dimensional system capable of moving/transforming the Citadel's relationship to dimensions.
- Rick's stolen/scanned Citadel schematics are specifically important to the Dimensional Drive.
- The exact full room geometry must be reconstructed from the relevant shots, not invented from the concept alone.

## Industrial/service levels
- Factories and utility facilities need machinery, access, loading/service circulation and security appropriate to their shown functions.
- Simple Rick's is a key reference for industrial scale.

## Ruined/exposed layers
- Destruction exposes interior structure and technical infrastructure.
- Ruin imagery can therefore be used as evidence for hidden layers that are not visible in intact city shots.

## Layering rule

When a source frame reveals a vertical relationship, record:
- layer name
- visible floor/level
- connection to adjacent layer
- access mechanism
- structural boundary
- machinery/utilities
- confidence
- exact source/scene
